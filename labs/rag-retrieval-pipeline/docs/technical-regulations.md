# Technical regulations (fictional series)

## Fuel
Maximum fuel flow is 100 kg per hour above 10,500 rpm. Fuel temperature must not be more than 10 degrees Celsius below ambient. A one-litre fuel sample must be available after the race.

## Tires
Each driver receives 13 sets of dry tires per weekend. Tire blankets may heat tires to a maximum of 70 degrees Celsius. Minimum starting pressures are published by the supplier before each event.

## Weight
The minimum weight of the car with driver is 798 kg. Ballast must be fixed so that tools are needed to remove it, and its position may be changed only in the presence of a scrutineer.
