# Sporting regulations (fictional series)

## Pit lane
The pit lane speed limit is 60 km/h during practice and 80 km/h during the race. A car exceeding the limit receives a drive-through penalty. Teams may change all four tires during a stop. Refuelling during the race is not permitted.

## Safety car
When the safety car is deployed, cars must slow to the delta time shown on the dashboard and may not overtake. Lapped cars may be instructed to unlap themselves before the restart. The pit lane remains open under the safety car unless race control closes it.

## Parc fermé
From the start of qualifying until the race, cars are under parc fermé conditions. Setup changes are limited to front wing angle and tire pressures. Changing a power unit component in parc fermé results in a pit lane start.
